The problem

Field surveys are hard for reasons that have little to do with the questions.

No connectivity

Surveyors work in remote areas with no reliable signal. Most digital tools simply stop working.

Questionable data

With thousands of responses, some will be rushed, mistaken or fabricated. Spotting them by hand is near impossible.

Coordination

Many surveyors, many forms, many projects at once — each person should only see what they're allowed to.

Renting, not owning

Hosted survey tools cap usage, keep your data on someone else's cloud, and bill you every year.

FAQ

Common questions

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. The Android and iOS apps store everything on the device and sync automatically when a connection is available. Nothing is lost if the phone restarts or the app is updated.

Where is our data stored?

On your own infrastructure. DataWave is deployed on your server, so your data stays in your country and under your control.

Are there limits on users or submissions?

No. Projects, surveyors and submissions are unlimited in every package.

Can it be customised for our workflow?

Yes. We scope custom features, such as analysis across survey rounds or special reports, in a requirements workshop with your team.

Which languages are supported?

The whole platform — every screen and form — works in English and Bangla, for staff and respondents alike.

Can we export to Excel, CSV or Stata?

Yes. Export to CSV, Excel and Stata, and import Stata files too.
